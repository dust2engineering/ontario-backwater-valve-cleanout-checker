Backwater valves (7.4.6.4)

The 2024 Code deleted the old "gate valve" option — Sentence (3) now requires a backwater valve.

Where cleanouts must go (7.4.7.1)

Above every running trap (upstream side) · inside leader at its base or within 1 m upstream (2024 tightened this from 3 m) · building drain at the exit point, NPS 4 or larger · stacks at the base, within 3 m upstream, or on the Y fitting · immediately downstream of an interceptor · indirect waste for food equipment at every turn over 45° · kitchen sink trap arm, close downstream of the trap. Maintenance holes: spacing ≤ 90 m (sewer ≤ NPS 24) or ≤ 150 m (larger); building to first maintenance hole ≤ 75 m developed length (2024 raised this from 30 m).
