Restaurant building drain, NPS 4:

$ python3 cleanout_checker.py --drain-nps 4

Drain NPS 4: minimum cleanout NPS 3; maximum spacing 15 m (one-way rodding) / 30 m (two-way). [Table 7.4.7.2, OBC 2024]

Note the trap for designers trained on the NPC: the national code wants a same-size (NPS 4) cleanout here; Ontario 2024 requires NPS 3.
